## nwtd-unrecordable (Nobody Writes This Down #2)

**Instagram:**
The demo was recorded. The lunch wasn't. Guess where the deal moved.

Meeting bots capture the scheduled parts of selling. Trust gets built in the unscheduled parts — the hallway, the walk to the parking lot, the second coffee.

What's the most important deal moment you never could have recorded?

**Facebook:**
Every tool in the conversation-intelligence category shares one blind spot: it can only capture conversations that happen where a bot is welcome.

The deal moved at lunch. No bot was invited. What's your version of that moment?

## nwtd-the-tape (Nobody Writes This Down #3)

**Instagram:**
Nobody minds being coached. Everybody minds being archived.

Feedback is a conversation. A recording is a permanent, searchable, shareable artifact — and reps adjust what they say accordingly. That adjustment has a cost, and it's paid in honesty.

Would your calls sound different without the tape running?

**Facebook:**
Reps don't fear feedback — they fear the permanent record. There's a difference between being coached and being archived, and everyone on your team can feel it.

Would your calls sound different without the tape running?

## carousel-no-upstream (thesis carousel #2)

**Instagram:**
Most sales AI produces output ABOUT you: scores, summaries, reports that travel up the org chart.

A thinking partner is a different design. What you work out together stays yours — because honest thinking needs a room with no audience.

Who reads what your AI writes about you?

**Facebook:**
Ask one question about any AI tool your company gives you: who reads its output?

If the answer is "someone above you," it's not a thinking partner — it's a reporting layer. Honesty needs a room with no audience. That's a design decision, not a feature.
